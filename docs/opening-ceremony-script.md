### CGI challenge / Défi CGI

#### English

**Afaq**

Our third main track is the CGI challenge.

The task is to build an AI-powered solution for complaint triage and response, along with an implementation plan for the fictional client.

Think about what happens when complaints come in. How does someone understand what each complaint is about? How can they organize it and work toward a useful response?

Then think beyond the demo. How would the client actually introduce your solution into their workflow? What would they need to make it useful?

The implementation plan is part of the challenge, so make time for it alongside your build. A good place to start is to map out the journey of one complaint from the moment it arrives to the point where someone responds.

*Garder la même diapositive. Ne pas avancer pendant la traduction.*
